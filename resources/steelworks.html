Steel Structure Types

Primary applications of structural steel in civil engineering

Multi-Story Buildings

Steel moment-resisting frames and braced frames for commercial and residential high-rises. Offers rapid construction, large open spans, and seismic resilience.

  • Moment frames & braced frames
  • Composite floor systems
  • Lightweight vs. heavy steel

Steel Bridges

Truss, arch, cable-stayed, and suspension bridges utilizing high-strength steel. Superior strength-to-weight ratio enables longer spans.
