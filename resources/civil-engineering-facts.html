IS Code Reference

📏
IS 1200 (Part 1 to 28)
Methods of Measurement of Building Works

Governs how quantities are measured — earthwork in excavation (Part 1), concrete (Part 2), brickwork (Part 3), woodwork (Part 4), etc. All quantities in this game follow IS 1200 rules.

🧱
IS 456 : 2000
Plain and Reinforced Concrete

RCC design parameters, concrete grades, steel cover, and reinforcement percentages. Used for RCC rate analysis and steel estimation.

⚙️
IS 800 : 2007
General Construction in Steel

Steel structural work measurement and costing. Used for structural steel rate analysis.

💰
DSR / CSR / PWD Schedule
Delhi / Central / State Schedule of Rates

Government-approved rates for labour, material, and machinery. Rates in this game are derived from DSR 2024 patterns.
