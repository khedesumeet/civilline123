How to Play

🎯 Objective

Complete 5 surveying challenges to become a master surveyor. Each level tests a different skill with a countdown timer.

📏 Level 1: Dumpy Level

Read staff measurements and calculate Reduced Levels (RL).
Formula: RL = HI - Staff Reading

🧭 Level 2: Theodolite

Measure horizontal angles between survey stations. Read the dial carefully and enter the angle.

📐 Level 3: Chain Survey

Measure distances with a chain and calculate the area of a survey plot (rectangle, square, triangle, trapezoid).

🗺️ Level 4: Contour Mapping

Identify all grid points that lie on the same elevation contour line from a 5×5 spot level grid.

🏆 Level 5: Final Survey

Combine all skills in a timed comprehensive survey task. Speed and accuracy matter!
